# Khabar AI — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 June 2026_

Khabar AI is a daily audio news briefing. This policy explains what we collect
and why. It is intentionally short and plain.

## What we collect

**Account.** When you sign in with Google (via Supabase), we store your account
identifier so we can show your saved stories and preferences. We do not store
your Google password.

**Usage analytics.** To understand how the app is used and to improve it, we
record product-analytics events — for example: opening the app, loading a
briefing, playing/pausing a story, completing a story, opening story details,
clicking a source, and changing language. Each event is associated with your
account identifier and basic context (platform, app version, language, and the
time it happened). We do **not** collect the content of your messages, contacts,
location, or any special-category personal data.

Analytics events are stored in our own database (Supabase) and also processed by
**PostHog**, a product-analytics provider acting as our processor, for
aggregate reporting such as funnels and retention.

## How we use it

- To operate core features (sign-in, saved stories, playback).
- To measure and improve the product in aggregate (which sections and stories
  are popular, listen-through rates, reliability).

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app
tracking.

## Your choices

Analytics is on by default and is disclosed here. You can opt out of analytics
at any time from the app's Settings; opting out stops new analytics events from
your device. You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

## Retention

Analytics events are retained only as long as needed for product analysis and
are not tied to any identity beyond your account identifier.
